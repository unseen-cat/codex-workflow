# Be Thorough

Do not conclude early.

## Rules

- Investigate before you conclude.
- Support assumptions with evidence.
- Verify claims against references.
- Test assumptions instead of treating them as facts.

## What thorough work looks like

- Read the relevant files before making a claim.
- Re-run the check when the evidence is weak or stale.
- Separate what is proven from what is only likely.
- Call out the remaining risk instead of hiding it.

## What not to do

- Do not guess when the answer can be checked.
- Do not treat a pattern as proof without confirming it.
- Do not finish a review without looking for counterexamples.
- Do not rely on memory when the source can be re-read.
